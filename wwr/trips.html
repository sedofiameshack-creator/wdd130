Featured Trips

Family Splash Float

Beginner

Perfect for families and first-timers, the Family Splash Float is a relaxing half-day journey through calm waters and gentle riffles. You will enjoy stunning canyon scenery, wildlife spotting, and a few small splashes along the way. No experience required — just bring your sense of adventure!

  • Duration: 4 hours
  • Difficulty: Class I – II
  • Min Age: 5 years
  • Price: $65 per person

Canyon Crusher

Intermediate

The Canyon Crusher takes you through dramatic gorges and exciting intermediate rapids for a full day of river fun. This is our most popular trip — it blends the thrill of real white water with breathtaking scenery that will leave you speechless. Lunch on the riverbank is included!

  • Duration: 8 hours (full day)
  • Difficulty: Class III – IV
  • Min Age: 12 years
  • Price: $120 per person

Extreme Surge

Advanced

Only for the most experienced and bold paddlers, the Extreme Surge conquers some of the most powerful Class V rapids in the region. This adrenaline-fueled multi-day expedition pushes your limits and rewards you with an experience few people on Earth ever get to have. Are you ready to surge?

  • Duration: 2 days / 1 night
  • Difficulty: Class V
  • Min Age: 18 years
  • Price: $290 per person
